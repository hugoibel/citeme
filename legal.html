3. Privacy

One-line summary: no accounts, no passwords, and the analysis is never stored on any server.

What happens when you press "Analyze my business"

  • The name, city and activity you type are sent to an intermediate server (Cloudflare Workers) which forwards them to Google's Gemini model with web search enabled.
  • The answer comes back to your browser and the report is drawn on your own device.
  • Neither the business, nor the city, nor the report are stored. Close the tab and they're gone.

The only thing that is stored

So nobody drains the service, the server keeps a request count:

WhatWhyHow long
Your IP address, used as a counter label Cap requests per visitor per day 48 hours, then auto-deleted
A global daily request counter Stay inside the API budget 48 hours

It's a number, not a history: it records how many queries an IP made, never which ones.

In your own browser

The site stores on your device (not on a server) your preferred language and, if you use advanced mode, your API key. Clearing the site data in your browser removes it.

No tracking cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels.
